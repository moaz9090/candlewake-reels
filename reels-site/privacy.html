Privacy Policy

Candlewake · Effective October 23, 2026

What this app is

The Candlewake publisher ("the app") is a private tool run by the owner of the Candlewake channel. It uploads short videos created for the channel to the Candlewake TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the channel owner signs in to it.

Information the app accesses

  • TikTok: when the account owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, TikTok provides an access token, a refresh token and basic profile information (such as the account's open ID and display name). The app uses the video.upload and video.publish permissions only to upload videos to that same account and to check their upload status.
  • Instagram: the app uses an access token for the owner's Instagram professional account to publish Reels to that account and read back the link to the published post.

The app does not collect, store or process any information about viewers, followers or other users.

How information is used

Tokens and profile identifiers are used only to publish the owner's videos to the owner's accounts. They are not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with anyone other than the platform they came from.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository that only the owner can access. They are masked in logs and never published.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner revokes them. Access can be revoked at any time from the app permissions section of TikTok's or Instagram's settings, after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @candlewake on TikTok or @candlewake1 on Instagram.
